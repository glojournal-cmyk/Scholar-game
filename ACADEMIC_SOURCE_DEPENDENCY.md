# Academic source dependency

The V0.3 handoff requires `Master_Content_Pack_V1` as the structured Year 8 source of truth, including conceptId, status, answer mode, mastery weights, review rules and Biology manual/checklist marking routes.

That structured pack was not available as an accessible file during this implementation. The available Year 8 Biology booklet PDFs are useful learning sources, but substituting them for the required structured pack would violate the handoff's instruction not to rebuild or regenerate the verified academic engine.

For that reason:
- Year 8 Biology appears under Foundation Review.
- Its Practice/Review route is intentionally unavailable.
- No Biology practice questions were invented or reconstructed.

To finish Phase 5 exactly, add the actual `Master_Content_Pack_V1` structured files and wire them through the existing Subject Hub without changing their academic fields.
