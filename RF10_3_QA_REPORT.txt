RF10.3 Stable Study Controls QA
===================================

Live issue
----------
Year 8 and Year 9 Learn/Practise/Play/Progress controls appeared enabled but could not be clicked.

Root cause fixed
----------------
The RF10 presentation MutationObserver repeatedly triggered decorateStudy(), which replaced
the action rail button DOM every refresh. Those replacements triggered the observer again.
The click target therefore never remained stable long enough for reliable pointer activation.

RF10.3 correction
-----------------
- Study action buttons are created only when subject/track changes.
- Existing button nodes are preserved on normal refreshes.
- The MutationObserver ignores mutations inside generated Study action rails.
- Status chips cannot intercept the action rail.
- app.js remains the single routing owner.
